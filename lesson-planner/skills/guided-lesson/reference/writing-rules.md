# Writing rules (binding)

## Level and tenses
- **Pre-A1 and A1: no past tenses anywhere**: texts, questions, distractors, hints, model answers.
- **A2 and above: nothing beyond what the class has been taught.** Ask what they know if you are not sure. A limit
  the teacher sets ("masculine singular only") applies to every sentence of the lesson.

## Questions
- Warm-up and speaking questions are personal but never intrusive. Topic "break-ups": ask "Is it easy for you to end
  a friendship?", never "Why did you break up with your ex?". No health, money troubles, religion, politics, sex or
  loss unless that is the lesson topic, and then ask about people in general.
- Short: one clause at Pre-A1–A1, at most two at A2.

## Texts and dialogues
- Length per level in `levels.md`. A little over is fine for the story; padding to fit words is not.
- **Dialogues have at most 2 speakers.** Three meetings = three dialogues of two people.
- **Real-life logic and order.** Someone who came for shoes is shown shoes first. A word that does not fit the
  story stays for the exercises.
- The text uses the lesson's words and grammar naturally. Every text has a picture. A podcast or audio input
  becomes a 2-speaker dialogue.
- Texts and dialogues are not translated: reading them is the exercise.

## Building a sentence
- **Write the answer first, then a natural sentence that contains it exactly once.**
- **Vocabulary gap fill: no hint.** `I want to buy a pair of shoes.` with answer `a pair of shoes`.
  Pre-A1 only: the scrambled word may be the hint (`hint: "gdo"` for `dog`).
- **Grammar gap fill: the infinitive (base form) in brackets**, carrying every word the answer needs:
  `She has (have) two kids.`, `Je n'ai plus écrit (ne plus écrire)…` → `hint: "ne plus écrire"`.
- Comprehension items test the text's content and words, never the grammar point.
- `test`: exactly one right option and 3 distractors that are **real words** of the same part of speech. Never invent words.
- Unscramble: at least 4 words; maximum per level in `levels.md`.
- **Memory games: 5 pairs at most.**

## Grammar
- The rule is always stated explicitly after the noticing step, in easy words, with examples.
- **Grammar tables stay in the target language only** and hold grammar, never word lists.

## Translations
- **Every exercise sentence and task has a translation** (`tr`) into the teacher's chosen language: tasks, test and
  gap sentences, unscramble, speaking questions, podcast host lines, theory slides, classify items and columns,
  sort_paragraphs paragraphs, picture_choice words.
- Not translated: texts, dialogues, grammar tables.
- Never translate into the language being learned.
